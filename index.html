About Me

Hi everyone, my name is Mo Kharboutli, and I am a passionate game developer. I have a deep love for creating games and coding. I am currently a student at Mediacollege Amsterdam, where I am trying my best to learn as much as I can about game development. I specialize in Unity and C#, but I would love to expand my skill set and learn more about game development in general. Ever since I was younger, I have always loved playing games, and I always wanted to create my own. I never really coded when I was younger; I started coding as soon as I began studying game development, and I have been loving it ever since. I am a very dedicated person, and I am always trying to improve my skills and learn new things. I'd like to think that I'm a very easy person to talk to, and I am always open to new opportunities and collaborations. So, if you want to work with me or just chat about game development, feel free to reach out to me!
