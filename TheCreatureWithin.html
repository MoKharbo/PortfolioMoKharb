How the team worked

The first thing we did was get to know eachothers and what we were good at. Once we found out what we were good at, we started to plan the tasks for each person so we could work on the project as efficiently as possible.

  • We did a standup every morning to discuss the day's goals and any blockers.
  • We shared progress often in a shared group chat to keep everyone informed.
  • We had a trello board to distribute tasks and track progress.

How I made the shooting

The shooting was made with a raycast that would detect if the player was aiming at an enemy. If the player was aiming at an enemy, the enemy would take damage and play an attack animation made by the artists. The shooting was made to feel quick and responsive, so the player could react fast and keep moving.

  • I set up the shooting logic so input felt immediate and easy to control.
  • I worked on the visual feedback so each shot looked clear on screen.
  • I balanced the mechanic through testing until it felt smooth in the project.
