How the team worked

This was the first project where I had to work with a team of developers and artists. I had to learn how to communicate with the team and how to plan tasks for each person. I also had to learn how to work with other people's code.

  • We made a trello board to distribute tasks and track progress.
  • We had a group chat to communicate and share updates.
  • We held regular meetings to discuss the project's progress and any issues that arose.

What I did

I worked on the car drifting mechanic, which was a key feature for this project.

  • I designed the drifting physics and implemented the mechanic in the game engine.
  • I worked on the visual effects to make the drifting look realistic and engaging.
